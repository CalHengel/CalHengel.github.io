## Description of Assignment

In this assignment, we are programming the LilyPad USB Plus to turn on the LEDs connected to pins 5, 6, 7 and 8. Each LED pin turns on when it matches its number in seconds elapsed. Each LED remains on until it is time for the next LED to turn on. This pattern repeats continuously on the LilyPad USB Plus until it is turned off. If/Else statements were used in the code on the Arduino program to determine which LED turns on.

## Tip/Hint to Give Past Self on This Deliverable

A tip I would give my past self while completing this deliverable is to go one LED at a time before trying to do all 4 at once. This way it will be easier to find mistakes in the pin numbers and If/Else statements.
